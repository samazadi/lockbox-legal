Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Lockbox ("we", "our", "the app") is a time capsule app that lets you save and share memories with friends. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Data We Collect

Account

  • Email address — used for passwordless sign-in (one-time passcode). We do not collect passwords.
  • Display name and avatar — set by you in your profile.

Content you create

  • Drops — photos, videos, voice recordings, and Spotify links you add to boxes.
  • Boxes — time capsule containers you create, including name, design choices, and settings.

Device information

  • Push notification token — so we can send you notifications about your boxes. Stored alongside your device platform (iOS/Android). Deleted when you log out.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Browsing or usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Crash reports

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session.
  • To store and deliver your drops and boxes to you and the members you share them with.
  • To send push notifications you have opted in to (new drops, box unlocked, etc.).

4. How We Store Your Data

All data is stored on Supabase infrastructure. Media files (photos, videos, voice recordings, avatars) are stored in Supabase Storage. Database records are stored in Supabase's managed PostgreSQL. Session tokens are stored locally on your device.

5. Who Can See Your Data

Your drops are visible only to members of the box you added them to. Boxes can be shared via invite or join code. Blind drops are anonymous to other box members.

We do not sell, share, or provide your data to any third parties.

6. Device Permissions

  • Camera — to take photos and videos for drops and your profile picture.
  • Microphone — to record voice drops.
  • Notifications — to alert you about box activity. Optional and configurable per box.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app. When you request account deletion, we remove your profile, drops, and associated data. Push notification tokens are deleted immediately when you log out.

8. Children's Privacy

Lockbox is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
